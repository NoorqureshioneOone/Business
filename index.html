Our Philosophy

Where art meets the art of dining

Nestled within one of the world's great museums, The Modern is conceived as a dining experience inseparable from its surroundings — a restaurant that breathes the same air as Picasso, Matisse, and Warhol.

Executive Chef Thomas Allan's cooking is refined, seasonal, and unexpectedly playful — a cuisine that rewards the curious palate with moments of genuine discovery, much like the galleries next door.

Overlooking the serene Abby Aldrich Rockefeller Sculpture Garden, every meal here is framed by beauty.

Chef Thomas Allan

Executive Chef · The Modern

Promoted to Executive Chef in 2020, Thomas Allan has brought a new voice to The Modern — one defined by refined restraint and an instinct for the unexpected. His menus are rooted in seasonality and exceptional sourcing, elevated through technique and imagination.

From the tasting room to the Kitchen Table, every dish is an exercise in balance: precision tempered by playfulness, luxury made approachable.

Thomas Allan
